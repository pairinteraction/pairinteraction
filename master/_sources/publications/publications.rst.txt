##############
 Publications
##############

If you use PairInteraction in your research, please cite our paper:

    Johannes Mögerle, Frederic Hummel, Alicia Keil, Tangi Legrand, Eduard J. Braun, Henri Menke, Jonathan King, Beatriz
    Olmos, Sebastian Hofferberth, Hans Peter Büchler, Sebastian Weber, *Accurate Modeling of Rydberg Atoms and Their
    Interactions: Theory and Implementation in PairInteraction*, `arXiv:2605.14993 <https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14993>`_

    .. code-block:: bibtex

        @misc{Moegerle2026,
            author = {M{\"o}gerle, Johannes and Hummel, Frederic and Keil, Alicia and Legrand, Tangi and Braun, Eduard J. and Menke, Henri and King, Jonathan and Olmos, Beatriz and Hofferberth, Sebastian and B{\"u}chler, Hans Peter and Weber, Sebastian},
            title = {{Accurate Modeling of Rydberg Atoms and Their Interactions: Theory and Implementation in PairInteraction}},
            year = {2026},
            doi = {10.48550/arXiv.2605.14993},
            url = {https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14993}
        }

If you would like to read more about the underlying theory and methods, we also refer to the original tutorial paper:

    Sebastian Weber, Christoph Tresp, Henri Menke, Alban Urvoy, Ofer Firstenberg, Hans Peter Büchler, Sebastian
    Hofferberth, *Tutorial: Calculation of Rydberg interaction potentials*, `J. Phys. B: At. Mol. Opt. Phys. 50, 133001
    (2017) <https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6455/aa743a>`_, `arXiv:1612.08053 <https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.08053>`_

publications for their atomic species alongside PairInteraction.
